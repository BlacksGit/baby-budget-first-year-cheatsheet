# First-Year Baby Budget: Free Cost Cheat Sheet

Rough US price ranges for a baby's first 12 months, plus a savings timeline and a hospital bill checklist. Prices vary by area and plan, so treat these as starting points.

## What a baby costs (first year)

| Item | Typical range | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Crib or bassinet | $80-$300 | Used is fine if it meets current safety standards |
| Infant car seat | $80-$250 | Never buy used with unknown crash history |
| Stroller | $60-$400 | Check local buy-nothing groups first |
| Diapers | $600-$900 | About 2,500 diapers, wipes extra |
| Formula or nursing supplies | $0-$1,500 | Wide range |
| Clothing (0-12 months) | $100-$300 | Buy few, buy used |
| Bottles, pump, feeding gear | $60-$250 | Insurance may cover a pump |
| Monitor | $30-$200 | Audio-only is plenty |
| High chair | $40-$200 | Needed around 6 months |
| Health copays and deductible | $500-$3,000 | Check your plan |
| Childcare | $0-$12,000+ | Largest line if both parents work |

## Three savings goals before the due date

1. **$1,000 starter fund** for the hospital and first purchases.
2. **Deductible fund** equal to your plan's out-of-pocket maximum.
3. **One extra month of expenses** to cover gaps in leave.

## Hospital bill checklist

- Confirm hospital, OB and pediatrician are in network before the due date.
- Ask for an itemized bill and check for duplicate charges.
- Add the baby to your plan within the enrollment window (often 30 days).
- Ask billing about financial assistance and interest-free payment plans.
- Wait for the insurance explanation of benefits before paying.
- Keep every bill, call date and name in one folder.

## Do not buy yet

Wipe warmers, newborn shoes, walkers, bulk wipes, nursery decor you will not use, anything that needs assembly in the last week.
